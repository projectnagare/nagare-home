# 0017 — Hosted Nagare is a complete bank sandbox

**Status:** Accepted

Nagare should eventually provide hosted isolated synthetic banks as a service.

A sandbox should expose fake customer/business identities, bank logins, APIs, webhooks, payment rails, bureau/KYC services, ledger state, Consumer/Business/Institutional surfaces, reset/replay, accelerated time and failure/scenario injection.

The hosted product is a programmable synthetic financial institution, not a set of mocked endpoints.
