# 0018 — Mature banking systems inform the domain, not the UX

**Status:** Accepted

Nagare should study Apache Fineract and other mature banking platforms for domain semantics, product concepts, accounting behavior and edge cases.

Nagare must not inherit legacy enterprise interaction patterns or architectural constraints merely because they exist in mature systems.

Domain maturity is an input. Nagare's architecture and UX are designed for a digital-only bank.
