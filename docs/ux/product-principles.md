# Product and UX Principles

Nagare should be modern at every layer.

The project may learn domain concepts from mature systems such as Apache Fineract, but it should not inherit their UX assumptions automatically.

## Consumer-grade internal software
The institutional experience deserves the same design attention as the customer experience.

## Work, not modules
Interfaces should organize around what a user needs to decide or complete.

## One design language
Consumer, Business, Institutional and Developer surfaces should share the Nagare design system while adapting to their context.

## Density where useful
Consumer experiences should remain calm and simple. Institutional experiences may be dense, fast and information-rich without becoming visually hostile.

## Design-system dependency
Nagare applications should consume the official packages from nagare-design-system rather than recreating tokens, icons or controls locally.
