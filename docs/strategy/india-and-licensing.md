# India and a Future Digital-Bank Licence

Project Nagare is designed to be useful before any standalone digital-bank licence exists in India.

The project must distinguish clearly between current law and regulation, historical policy proposals, and architecture designed for a possible future regime.

If a suitable digital-bank licensing regime emerges, Nagare's objective is to move from synthetic to production infrastructure by replacing integrations, configuring regulatory constraints, completing certification and operating a controlled launch.

The software architecture should not assume that Consumer and Business necessarily share one licence or legal entity.

Licensing assumptions should be documented, versioned and revisited as regulation changes.
