# Cost Thesis

Nagare is interested in the economics of operating a bank, not only the elegance of its software.

A central question is:

> How close can the cost of financial intermediation get to the actual cost of capital, risk and infrastructure?

The synthetic bank should eventually measure cost per active customer, transaction, loan originated, ₹1 crore of assets, operational case and AI action, along with fraud losses, credit losses, net interest margin and cost-to-income ratio.

This makes operating efficiency an engineering problem that can be measured and improved.
