# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Constitution
Establish the principles, architecture, decisions, RFC process and design system.

## Phase 1 — First vertical slice
Prove one complete banking flow: create a synthetic customer, open an account, receive salary from a synthetic employer, make a payment to a synthetic merchant, route through a simulated payment rail, post correctly to the ledger, and display the result in the Consumer interface.

## Phase 2 — Consumer and Business foundations
Build independent Consumer and Business distributions on the same substrate.

## Phase 3 — Institutional bank
Add treasury, risk, finance, compliance, fraud, operations and regulatory capabilities.

## Phase 4 — Hosted sandbox
Allow external builders to create, seed, reset and operate synthetic Nagare environments.

## Phase 5 — Production readiness
Build production adapters, certification tooling, operational controls and licensing profiles as relevant regulation emerges.
