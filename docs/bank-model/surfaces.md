# Consumer, Business, Institutional and Developer

Nagare has four major experience surfaces.

## Consumer
The individual customer's view of the bank: accounts, deposits, cards, payments, savings, credit and servicing.

## Business
The business customer's view of the bank: KYB, current accounts, payments, payroll, receivables, payables, cards, working capital, cash management and treasury services.

## Institutional
The bank's own view of itself: treasury, ALM, liquidity, capital, finance, risk, compliance, fraud, regulatory reporting, audit, operations, servicing and internal approvals.

Institutional is not another customer segment. It is the bank's operating surface.

## Developer
The platform and sandbox view used to integrate with, extend and test Nagare.

All four surfaces should share one design language while adapting appropriately to their context.
