# AI in Nagare

Nagare is **AI-native, not AI-dependent**.

AI-native means machine actors are first-class participants in the institutional architecture. It does not mean every action invokes a model.

## AI actors are plugins

An AI actor should have identity, capabilities, permissions, tools, task boundaries, policy constraints, supervision, audit trail and model/runtime configuration.

Examples may include an AI credit reviewer, treasury analyst, fraud investigator, AML investigator, collections officer, support agent or operational-risk analyst.

Banking capabilities must not depend on a specific AI implementation.

The institution should continue to function correctly if every AI plugin is removed.
