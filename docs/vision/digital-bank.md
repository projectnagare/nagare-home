# What Nagare Means by a Digital Bank

A digital bank is not merely a conventional bank with good mobile banking.

Nagare uses the term to mean an institution whose operating model assumes digital interaction from first principles across customer banking and the institution itself.

That includes onboarding, servicing, payments, lending, collections, fraud, compliance, treasury, liquidity, risk, finance, regulatory reporting, operations, audit and internal approvals.

The architectural consequence is simple:

> **The bank is the branch.**

Nagare should not organize its internal model around physical branches. If external systems or regulation require branch codes or similar identifiers, they can be represented through adapters and compatibility fields.

A digital bank may still have people, offices, call centres, legal entities and operational teams. What it does not require is a branch-centric system architecture.
